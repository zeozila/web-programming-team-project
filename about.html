The idea

One website, two directions

Most travel guides are written for Western tourists. When we travel between Korea and Mongolia, we end up juggling blogs, a translator, a currency app and a weather app.

SeoulBaatar puts all of it in one place and works both ways: choose Korea or Mongolia at the top and the places, phrases, money, weather and quiz all switch to that country. The name mixes Seoul and Ulaanbaatar — and baatar means "hero" in Mongolian.

What's inside

Features

Two-way destination switch

Pick Korea or Mongolia and every page adapts: photos, places, language order, currency and weather.

Interactive map

26 places on a Leaflet map. Filter by country, interest or month and the pins update live.

Phrasebook + flashcards

41 phrases in Korean and Mongolian with romanization, audio and a flashcard trainer.

Live money & weather

Real exchange rates and a 5-day forecast from free public APIs.

Trip planner

Budget calculator, packing checklist and saved places — all remembered in your browser.

Culture quiz

Ten questions on manners, with explanations and your best score saved.
